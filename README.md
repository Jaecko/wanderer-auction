# Wanderer Auction

The auction house in one quiet Wanderer window, made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 1.4 or later.

- Sell, Buy, Following, History and Shopping in one window that replaces the game's (one click brings the game's back).
- A fair price offered (optimized by default, bait left out), never one that would bring less than a merchant once the auction house's cut is taken; prices in whole silver.
- A guard against a costly slip: a price far under or over an item's usual value is asked before posting. Your own price can be kept per item.
- Sell everything in one card, a gesture per sale; take back your undercut auctions one click at a time, the deposit said first.
- The game's categories in a tree, your last searches, usual prices in green or red; every purchase and bid confirmed.
- A shopping list you can open anywhere, filled from your bags, quests and profession windows (with what a recipe costs), bought at merchants with +N or on arrival if you wish.
- What you set aside in [Wanderer Bags](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer-bags) comes first; what you keep is never offered.

Download: [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wanderer-auction). More on [wanderer-addon.com](https://wanderer-addon.com).
