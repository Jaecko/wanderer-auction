# Wanderer Auction

The auction house in one quiet Wanderer window, made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 0.40 or later.

- To sell, to buy, your auctions and your bids, in one window that replaces the game's (one click brings the game's back).
- The lowest price looked up for you, a price just below offered, never one that would bring less than a merchant once the auction house's cut is taken.
- What you set aside in [Wanderer Bags](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer-bags) comes first; what you keep is never offered.
- Prices remembered from your last visit, shown in Wanderer Bags and in the tooltips.

Download: [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wanderer-auction). More on [wanderer-addon.com](https://wanderer-addon.com).
