# Wanderer Auction 0.2.0

### New
- A shopping list: items added from Wanderer Quests or from Wanderer's reminders, searched all at once on the buying page (Shopping), cheapest first; what is not on sale said; a right click takes an item off it; what you already carry enough of leaves it by itself. Searched by itself when you open the buying page with nothing searched yet.
- Identical items (the same item, the same make) on one line to sell, posted together like a stack, each at the price you ask.

### Changed
- The cheapest auction of an item always comes first, whatever the order of the list.
- Your own sale no longer replaces the market's price when you post above it.
- The prices seen are forgotten after 30 days (lighter at login).
- Lighter: the auction house's events are heard only while it is open.

### Fixed
- Hiding the interface (Alt+Z) no longer closes the auction house.
- A question asked over another no longer answers for it; questions left open close with the auction house.
- The bidder on your auctions shows by name.
