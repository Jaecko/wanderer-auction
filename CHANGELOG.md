# Wanderer Auction 1.3.10

### Changed
- The most spent per visit to a merchant, for the shopping list, is also in the settings, under buying on arrival.
- Prices are typed in three boxes, gold, silver and copper, digits only (no more letters to type).
- The Sell page rebuilt, plainer: the item, one price, how many, how long, the total, one big button (Space) and Next (Shift+Space).
- What is for sale now, one line per price with how many are sold at it: a click on a line puts yours at that price.
- One place advised: at the price of the cheapest offer worth matching, or between a lone cheap offer and the next ones when the gap is wide, never selling it off.
- Prices kept to the copper: a click on 1s 35c posts at 1s 35c (whole silver only if the auction house refuses copper, said once).
- Lines a merchant beats are grey and still clickable: you are asked before posting at such a price.
- More reliable: one price search at a time, sent only when the auction house is ready, asked again when it does not answer; the button waits while the auction house is busy and never posts twice.
- The duration you choose is kept for the next sales (8 hours at first, the middle choice).
- The Following tab is now My auctions.
- Buy: a price well under or over its usual one says by how much in front of it, in green or red (-25 %, +40 %).
- Sell: what you receive once the cut is taken is shown, the deposit clearer; an item that cannot be sold says why when clicked.
- Buy: how many in each auction and the price each; the bid says whether it is the current one, the minimum, or yours.
- My auctions: a sale someone bid on shows the bid; one without a buyout is said, never blank.
- The starting bid is 95% of your buyout and shown in the money card.
- Drop an item from your bags on the window to choose it for sale.
- Next is greyed on the last item; rarities switched off and prices a merchant beats stay readable; the key word on the gold button is easier to read.
- The shopping list window opens and closes with a sound; "Add to the shopping list" and "Search an item...".

### Removed
- The optimized price, Sell everything, Look up the prices, the History tab and the journal.

### Fixed
- Buy page: Shift and a click on an auction links the item, Ctrl and a click tries it on, as in the game's list.
- Enter no longer posts an item: only Space or the button do, so pressing Enter to chat is safe.
- Space right after a sale or a bag move never posts the wrong item; Post waits for the prices to be known unless you typed one.
- Escape leaves every number box; Shift+click on the shopping list no longer opens the game's stack splitter too.
- A new character no longer starts buying your shopping list at merchants on its own: switch it on for that character.
- Taking back an auction cancels only the auction the card shows.
- Shift and a click on an item of your bags puts its name in the Buy page's search and searches it, from any page of the window (unless you are writing in the chat).
- The window you switch to by its link (Wanderer's or the game's) is the one that opens at your next visits, Auctionator there or not.
