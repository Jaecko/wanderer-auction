# Wanderer Auction 0.1.0

First release, an extension of Wanderer (0.40 or later).

### New
- The auction house in one Wanderer window, drawn like every Wanderer window: to sell, to buy, your auctions, your bids. It replaces the game's window; a link brings the game's back, another returns to Wanderer's.
- To sell: your bags in order, what Wanderer Bags sets aside for the auction house first, what you keep never offered. The lowest price looked up, a price just below offered, a starting bid for items, the deposit, the duration; one click posts it.
- Never below a merchant: a price that would bring less than a merchant gives, once the auction house's cut is taken, is not offered and cannot be posted; what you would receive is shown against the merchant's price, and a deposit greater than your gain is said.
- To buy: a search by name, the game's categories, filters and levels, sorted by price, name or level, your favorites; commodities bought by the quantity, items by buyout or bid, each confirmed first; what a merchant gives for it shown.
- Your auctions: the ones undercut said in red (and when following is not worth it), sold ones awaiting the mail, a cancel with its cost asked first.
- Your bids: the ones outbid first, a new bid or the buyout in one click, confirmed first.
- Prices remembered from your last visit, for the whole account: in Wanderer Bags and in the tooltips (when Auctionator is not there to say them).
- Right click an item of your bags at the auction house: chosen to sell. Shift+click a link: searched.
- Its own page in Wanderer's options.
