# Wanderer Auction 1.3.13

### Added
- Selling: how many others sell at the cheapest price, next to the usual price.

### Changed
- The advised price no longer follows a cheapest price held by one or two sellers alone when enough prices stand above (often a lure to make you sell lower): the next price instead.

# Wanderer Auction 1.3.11

### Changed
- With a controller, the Sell page's price steps up and down as the bid does.
- A bid you typed stays when the list refreshes, while it is still enough.
- Buying "all" with nothing left on sale: the button rests, and says why.
- With the game's look (a choice under Wanderer's Appearance): the categories and an item's auctions drawn with the game's own parts, its lit and chosen rows, its dividers and colors. Needs Wanderer 1.4.89.

### Fixed
- Posting under the market price: the wait for the house's answer starts from your yes, so a second post can no longer slip through; a no only cancels its own sale.
- Post: its words on the left of the button, as on Next, so the key beside them no longer runs over them.
