# Wanderer Auction 1.2.0

Needs Wanderer 1.4 or later.

### New
- A price guard: a price under half of an item's usual value, or over three times, is said in red and asked before posting, Sell everything included.
- Your price, kept per item: "Keep as your price" makes it the least ever offered for that item, on your whole account; "Forget your price" lets the market lead again.
- Take back the undercut: on the Following tab, your auctions cheaper elsewhere and worth reposting, in one card, canceled one by one with a click each, the deposit lost said first.
- In a profession's window, the "To the shopping list" button's tooltip says what the reagents cost and what the item sells for once the cut is taken, with the profit or the loss (from the prices you have seen).
- Your last eight searches under the Buy page's search box: a click searches again, a right click forgets.
- One price field: type a price as you like (1.5 is 1g 50s, 1g50s, 75s), match the lowest, step it 5 % down or up (Ctrl+Up, Ctrl+Down); a line says why that price.
- The way a price is offered is a setting: optimized (the default), sell fast, or at the market's price.
- Buy: the game's categories in a tree, your search within the one chosen, rarity chips, Usable only; a price in green or red against its usual price (14 days).
- Following: "Look up their prices" judges your auctions against the lowest price of the hour, never an old one.
- Sell everything in a card: Space or Enter posts the next item, S skips it; a key binding posts when the game wants a click.

### Changed
- Prices in whole silver: Forever's server refuses copper.
- The shopping list opens on a card per line (how many, always on you, your highest price), keys 1 to 4.
- At merchants, your shopping list is bought through Wanderer (whole lots, never more than the merchant has); Wanderer's provisions leave your list's items to it.
- "Better at a merchant: made junk" (with Wanderer Bags): uncommon or better is sold only by your click on the junk button.
- The search's filters and sort are each character's own; your last way and duration are kept.
- The window is 1040 by 640 at least, so the price row and filters fit in every language.

### Fixed
- A search answered while you looked at another tab is read when you come back, no longer "Searching..." for ever.
- Buying on arrival can no longer buy the list twice when a merchant is closed and opened again quickly.
- Identical items are posted one after the other when selling everything.
- A wide search no longer asks the server for hundreds of items at once.
- A Shift+click meant for the chat no longer asks "How many?" for the shopping list.
