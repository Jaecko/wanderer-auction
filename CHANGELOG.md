# Wanderer Auction 0.3.0

### New
- A new auction house window: Sell, Buy, Follow (your auctions and bids together) and Shopping list, sortable tables, the game's own durations (2, 8 or 24 hours). Resizable by its corner, its size kept.
- On the selling page, "Look up the prices" finds the price of all your items one by one, how far it is said as it goes.
- Your shopping list anywhere, in a small window: from the bags' heading (Wanderer Bags), a tab on the merchant's window, Wanderer's menu, a key of yours or /wauction. A click changes how many you want, a Shift+click on an item adds it.
- Lines "always on you": a count you always want to carry (reagents, ammunition, food). They never leave the list and are filled up at each merchant.
- At a merchant who sells items of your list, a "+N" link beside them buys exactly what is missing. An option (off) buys it all on arrival, for gold only, never above a cap per visit (5 gold by default), Shift skipping it. Also from the shopping list's window.
- In a profession's window, "To the shopping list" sends the reagents you lack for the recipe shown (as many times as asked).
- Items leave the list by themselves once you carry enough, wherever they came from; their tooltip says "Shopping list: 3 / 20".

### Changed
- Items your quests ask you to carry are never offered for sale.
- Needs Wanderer 0.42 or later.
